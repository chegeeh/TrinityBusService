Simple Process

How to Book

Booking your seat with Trinity Bus Service takes less than 2 minutes. Here's how:

01
🗺️

Choose Route & Departure

Select your origin, destination, and travel date, then pick either the 3:00 PM or 9:00 PM departure.

02
💺

Pick Your Seats

Choose your exact seat(s) on the live seat map, fill in your details, then confirm via WhatsApp on +254 751 277 900. You can also call +254 100 256 761 or email trinitybusservice.ke@gmail.com.

03
✅

Confirm & Travel

Receive your booking confirmation, pay, and show up at the departure point. Pack light and travel with peace of mind.
